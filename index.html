ANCHOR

Your week stays yours

Privacy policy for the Anchor app. Last updated October 16, 2026.

Anchor is a planner. Everything you put into it (tasks, your week, clients, notes, photos, reminders) is saved on your phone and nowhere else.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, blocks, client names and notes, session marks, photos you attach, your cheerleader lines and your settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. We do not run a server, so we never receive them and cannot see them.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is needed.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Permissions, and why

  • Face ID (optional, can be turned off in Settings): to open the app. iOS handles it; the app never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Camera and Photos: only when you add a photo to a session note. The photo is copied into the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded.
  • Notifications: only when you switch on a gentle reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and carry only the words you wrote.

Things you choose to send

The Export, Accountant CSV and Send buttons open the iOS share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is your choice. We never see it.

App updates

When you open Anchor, it may check for and download new versions of its own code from Expo's update service. Like any internet request, this reveals your device's IP address and the app's version to that service. It does not include anything you have entered in the app.

Deleting your data

Delete a client, task or note in the app to remove it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Because the data lives only on your phone, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

Anchor does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
